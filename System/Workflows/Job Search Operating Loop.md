# Job Search Operating Loop

Use this loop weekly:

1. Capture source material: resume, roles, conversations, target-company notes, and strategy artifacts.
2. Process source material into reviewable notes.
3. Decide what deserves action: apply, seek referral, research, hold, or skip.
4. Act in the real world.
5. Reconcile the notes after action.
6. Review signals weekly and update Candidate-Market Fit.

The system should help you see fit and momentum. It should not make final decisions for you.
