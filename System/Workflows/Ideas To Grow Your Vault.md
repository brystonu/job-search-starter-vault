# Ideas To Grow Your Vault

None of these are built, on purpose. Your search is not our search, and the best way to learn what an AI coding assistant can do is to build the piece *you* actually need, when you need it.

Each idea below is a problem statement plus a starter prompt. When one matches a friction you feel, copy the prompt into your AI assistant (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, or similar) opened in this vault folder. The assistant will follow the rules in [[Vault Evolution]]: smallest change, explained, reversible.

## 1. A fit score that matches your gut

**Problem:** The generic fit scoring keeps disagreeing with how you actually feel about roles.

> Ask: "Look at my last five application notes and what I wrote about them in my weekly reviews. Propose a fit-scoring rubric that matches how I actually judge roles, and update the Job Fit Scoring prompt to use it."

## 2. Resume variants per family

**Problem:** You tailor your resume from scratch every time.

> Ask: "Using my Resume Evidence Bank and my resume families, draft one resume variant per family I actually target. Keep every claim traceable to the evidence bank."

## 3. Interview prep packs

**Problem:** Interview prep starts from a blank page.

> Ask: "For the application note I point you at, build a prep pack: likely questions from the posting, my best evidence-bank stories for each, and the three claims I should verify before I say them out loud."

## 4. Follow-up cadence

**Problem:** Conversations go stale because follow-ups live in your head.

> Ask: "Scan my conversation notes for missing follow-ups, add a next-touch date field to the conversation template, and draft the two most overdue follow-up messages for my review."

## 5. Weekly review auto-draft

**Problem:** The weekly review feels like homework, so it gets skipped.

> Ask: "Draft this week's review from what changed in my vault this week - new notes, updated applications, conversations. Leave the judgment sections blank for me."

## 6. Market language diff

**Problem:** Postings describe your job in words your resume never uses.

> Ask: "Compare the language in the postings I've saved against my Canonical Resume. List the vocabulary gaps and suggest which Candidate-Market Fit hypotheses they support or contradict."

## 7. Company research briefs

**Problem:** You walk into conversations knowing less than you'd like.

> Ask: "Create a company-brief template for my vault, then fill one in for the company note I point you at, marking anything you couldn't verify as a claim to check."

## 8. A momentum view

**Problem:** You can't see at a glance whether the search is moving.

> Ask: "Using the metrics fields in my weekly reviews, add a simple momentum note (or an Obsidian Base view) that shows applications, conversations, and follow-ups over the last four weeks."

## 9. A job search pipeline

**Problem:** Working a posting end to end - capture, score, decide, tailor - takes too many manual steps, and you want your assistant to carry it from URL to a ready-to-review decision.

> Ask: "I want a job-search pipeline: give you a posting URL, you capture the posting, score it against my own criteria, help me decide whether to apply, and tailor my resume if I do. First, check what already exists here - Job Posting Intake and the Job Application template may already cover capture. Then look at my existing application notes and weekly reviews for a pattern in how I've actually judged roles, the way idea #1 does. Check with me: does that match how I really weigh things, what's missing, what does 'yes, apply' vs. 'hold' vs. 'skip' look like for me, and can you fetch a URL yourself or do I need to paste the text? Across capture, scoring, deciding, and tailoring, tell me which piece is actually missing or weakest for me right now, and build only that one, per Vault Evolution's one-change-at-a-time rule. Stop after that, tell me what you built and how to undo it, and wait for me to come back for the next piece once I've used it for a while. Never invent evidence, and keep application decisions and outreach mine to send."

This joins ideas #1 and #2 above into one flow, plus posting capture and a decision policy in between. Start with #1 alone if you want the smallest possible first step.

## Inventing your own

The pattern behind every idea above: **name the friction, point at the evidence in your vault, ask for the smallest change.** If you can fill in this sentence, you have a quest: "Every week I ___, and I wish the vault would ___."
