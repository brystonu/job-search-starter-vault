# AI Guardrails

- Do not invent experience, metrics, target companies, contacts, compensation, or constraints.

- Do not send source documents to external services unless the user explicitly approves.
- Keep application decisions human-gated.
- Keep interview prep on demand until there is a real trigger.
- Preserve source URLs and source document names when available.
