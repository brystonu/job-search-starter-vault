## Week 2 And Beyond

This vault is yours to change, and your AI assistant can do the heavy lifting. Climb one rung at a time:

1. Use the vault as-is for a week and note what annoys you (the weekly review asks).
2. Edit one template by hand.
3. Ask your AI assistant to change one prompt or template for you.
4. Pick an idea from [[Ideas To Grow Your Vault]] and build it together.
